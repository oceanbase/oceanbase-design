Through reasonable information hierarchy design, straightforward and unified concept expression, and timely feedforward guidance, users can quickly identify key points, understand product concepts, know what they can do, and find the content they need.

## Clear Hierarchy

Through reasonable information organization hierarchy, interaction flow, and visual hierarchy design, highlight important information and operations, and guide users to quickly find the content they need.

## Stay Consistent

Concepts with the same meaning and similar operations should remain consistent. Follow users' existing mental models and usage habits as much as possible to reduce understanding and learning costs.

## Timely Guidance

When users may encounter cognitive barriers, provide feedforward guidance through first-time experience guides, product concept and value explanations, and explanations for non-operable states. Answer users' questions and help them overcome obstacles faster.
