Follow internationalization design standards to design products that can adapt to multiple languages and regions, ensuring products can be used smoothly by users worldwide.

## Universal Visual Elements

Use culturally neutral colors, icons, and images. Avoid culture-specific symbols that may cause misunderstanding or offense. Ensure colors, images, and icons have the same meaning across different language environments.

## Reasonable Layout

Through reasonable layout, space reservation, and responsive design rules, adapt to different languages and screen sizes. Avoid text truncation or layout breakage.
